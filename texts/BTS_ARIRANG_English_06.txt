# ALBUM: ARIRANG

## TRACK: No. 29

### LANGUAGE: English

From BTS-TRANS/BANGTAN SUBS:
The bell heard in this track is the Sacred Bell of King Seongdeok, officially designated the 29th
National Treasure of Korea. RM mentioned in BTSʼ Weverse LIVE STUDIO NOTES | ‘ARIRANGʼ that
the track is 1:37 long as that is the length of time needed for the sound to die out completely after the
bell has been struck.
